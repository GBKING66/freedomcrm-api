## Slide 5 — The Solution

**Two apps. One system.**

- **PM App** — iPhone-first. PMs add a house, get an auto-generated task list, check off tasks as they go, and the system emails vendors and requests POs automatically. Nothing gets missed.
- **Management Snapshot** — Lou or Scott types in an address, sees status + delay reasons in 3 seconds. No login gymnastics.

Built specifically for **custom** home building. Not a compromise.

*Visual suggestion: side-by-side mock of PM app on iPhone and Management Snapshot on desktop.*

---

## Slide 6 — How It Works (Walkthrough)

1. **PM adds a house** — address + series (Encore, Reserve, Fort Worth Tier)
2. **App auto-generates the task list** for that series — everything in order, with vendors pre-assigned
3. **PM checks off tasks** as they complete
4. **App auto-emails the next vendor** — "Ready for [task] at [address]"
5. **App auto-requests POs** from purchasing when needed
6. **PM logs any delays** with reason (weather, owner approval, back-order, etc.)
7. **Management sees the snapshot** update in real time

Nothing gets dropped. Everything is logged. History is preserved.

*Visual suggestion: 3–4 screenshots in a numbered flow.*

## Slide 7 — What Management Sees

Type an address. See:

- Current phase (e.g., "Trim / paint prep")
- Estimated completion date (with slip from original in red if any)
- Current blocker + reason (e.g., "Delayed 5 days — waiting on cabinet plans")
- Who to call if you want more detail (PM name + one-tap dial)

**That's it.** No workflow, no clicks. If Lou wants more, he calls the PM.
