# Olerio Homes — Electrical Standards

These specs apply to **every Olerio Homes build**. Send a copy to the electrical vendor at project start and verify on every house walk.

---

## ELE-01 — Plugs above cabinets must be centered

Any electrical plug installed above a cabinet must be centered on **both axes**:

- **Left-right:** plug centered on the drawer directly below it.
- **Front-back:** plug centered on the depth of the cabinet.

Walk every plug above a cabinet on every house and relocate any that don't meet this spec.
