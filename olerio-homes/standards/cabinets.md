# Olerio Homes — Cabinet Standards

These specs apply to **every Olerio Homes build**. Send a copy to the cabinet vendor at project start and verify on every house walk.

---

## CAB-01 — Kitchen cabinets: complete stain grade

All kitchen cabinets must be **complete stain grade** — every component (boxes, doors, drawer fronts, face frames, end panels, fillers, toe kicks, crown, light rails, etc.) built from stain-grade material. No paint-grade wood anywhere in the kitchen, even on areas that will be hidden or covered.
